Platform overview

The metrics below were recorded from a single evaluation run on that synthetic data and have not been validated against real institutional performance. Random Forest was fixed at first build although Logistic Regression scored higher on every recorded metric. Estimates are advisory only.

How estimates are used

  • Estimates use attendance and continuous assessment data recorded before final examinations.
  • Final examination results are excluded from the feature set to avoid target leakage.
  • Estimates are advisory only and never automatically fail, suspend or disqualify a student.
  • Students and advisers can see the recorded factors behind each estimate.
Model output is decision support. Official academic decisions are made by authorised institution staff.
